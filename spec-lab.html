Harry Potter can give JioHotstar 30 days of daily users

HBO's Harry Potter series premieres this Christmas, and JioHotstar already streams all 8 films. This spec campaign uses the two months before it to build a daily habit.

The spell. Open JioHotstar, tap voice search and say "Lumos" to unlock a hidden Harry Potter Halloween zone inside the app.
New users. A secret worth sharing, a feature that is its own ad, and offline trivia nights with District and Swiggy Scenes.
Every day. One trivia question daily from 1 to 30 November. Keep the streak, with a Time-Turner to save it, to win HBO Max subscriptions, merch and a premiere invite.
