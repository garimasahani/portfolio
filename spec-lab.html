SaaS brands don't do Diwali. Sarvam should.

Most SaaS products have no brand identity and no personality, and festivals pass without a campaign. Sarvam is the exception. Its customers walk into the Diwali sales rush with more support calls, more offers to run, more users to nudge and more leads to chase. They need extra hands, and voice agents are exactly that.

The fit. Sarvam screams India, and India screams Diwali. Few B2B brands have that kind of natural fit.
The offer. "Launch your Diwali sale voice line in 24 hours." Delayed-order calls, offer reminders and lead qualification, all in the customer's own language.
Second cohort. Creators: one Diwali reel, dubbed into 11 languages.
Scale. It doesn't need to be big. A small campaign aimed at businesses would work.

Making Download for offline feel like a Premium perk again

Downloads are the second biggest reason people pay for Premium, after ad-free music. Yet in my survey of 26 Premium users (18–35, students and professionals), most found the feature clumsy. I mapped two segments, On-the-go listeners and Music lovers, and designed two A/B tests.

Test 1 · Button visibility. A prominent download button on the track page lifts download initiation by 15%.
Test 2 · Content management. Smart downloads and auto-expire cut time spent managing by 20% and lift satisfaction 10%. Ship if p < 0.05.

Spotify Premium, through the lens of how people actually decide

One brief, three moments. Before purchase I used emotional storytelling and social proof. During purchase I cut decision load with Hick's Law and created ownership with the endowment effect. After purchase I built habit loops that reward a daily routine.

Ad film · "No Ads, Just Perfect Timing." A surprise birthday, ruined by a car-insurance ad. Tagline: Kahi bhi, Kabhi bhi.
Forgotten Tracks. One nostalgic song a day, chosen from your old favourites, playable in one tap. A single choice in place of a hundred.
Habit titles. Cue → routine → reward. Play your gym playlist every day and earn "Workout Champ".
Pricing psychology. Charm pricing at ₹129, a Disney+ Hotstar bundle at ₹149, and one-tap UPI checkout.

Campaign Chronicles · Ep 1CRMPush copy

The Olympics push I'd actually tap

During Paris 2024 I got a JioCinema notification about live matches. I'm a big sports fan and it still didn't land. So I took it apart and rewrote the push strategy to bring more people to the stream: tie each push to a moment, an athlete and the viewer's own history.

Moments over menus. Name the event and the minute it starts.
Faces over formats. People tune in for athletes, so lead with the athlete's name.
Use what you know. Personalise using sports the viewer already watched.
Recover the miss. Turn a missed live event into a highlights tap.
